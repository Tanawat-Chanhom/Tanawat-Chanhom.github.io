A full rebuild of a mid-size storefront that had accumulated eight years of
plugins and no performance budget.

## Constraints

Search rankings could not regress, so every URL had to survive the migration
and Core Web Vitals had to improve from day one.

## Approach

Static generation for catalogue pages, client islands only where the cart
genuinely needed interactivity, and a checkout flow rewritten around real
keyboard and screen-reader testing rather than assumptions.

## Result

Lighthouse performance went from 41 to 98, and checkout completion rose 12%
in the first quarter after launch.

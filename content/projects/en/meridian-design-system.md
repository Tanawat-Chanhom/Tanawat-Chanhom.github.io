Four teams, four button components, four different focus styles. Meridian was
the attempt to make that stop.

## Scope

A token pipeline that emits CSS custom properties, a component library built
on top of it, and Storybook as the shared review surface between design and
engineering.

## What mattered most

Adoption. A design system nobody uses is just a second codebase to maintain,
so the migration path had to be incremental — teams could adopt one component
at a time without a big-bang rewrite.

## Result

Three of four teams fully migrated within two quarters, and accessibility
defects reported against shared components dropped to near zero.
